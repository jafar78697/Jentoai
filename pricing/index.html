Knowledge Base

Common Questions.

Is there a monthly subscription?

We primarily focus on build-to-own models where you pay for the architecture upfront. Ongoing costs are typically tiny API usage fees paid directly to providers (Google/n8n/Pinecone).

How long does a typical build take?

Starter nodes take 1-3 days. Enterprise transformations depend on complexity but usually range from 2-4 weeks for full deployment.

Can I monitor what the agents are doing?

Yes. Our n8n architecture provides a visual execution log. You can see every thought, decision, and action taken by your agents in real-time.

Is my data secure?

Completely. We specialize in "Siloed Architecture" where your data never leaves your private cloud instance or vector database. We never use your data to train generic AI models.
