Deep Dive Q&A.

What is business process automation?

Business process automation (BPA) uses technology to automate repetitive tasks and workflows. Instead of manually entering data, sending emails, or updating spreadsheets, AI agents and workflow tools like n8n can handle these tasks 24/7 with perfect accuracy.

Do I need technical skills to manage Jento Agents?

Not at all. We build "Black Box" solutions. You simply interact with the agent via chat or a simple dashboard. We handle all the API connections, hosting, and maintenance. It is like hiring a highly skilled employee who needs zero supervision.

Will AI replace my current team?

No. Jento Agents are designed to remove "grunt work"—data entry, scheduling, lead filtering—so your human team can focus on strategy, creative work, and closing deals. Our clients typically see their team happiness increase because burnout tasks are eliminated.

Does this work with my legacy/old software?

Yes. n8n is extremely flexible. If your software has an API, we connect directly. If not, we can build custom scrapers or database connectors. We act as the universal bridge between your modern tools (Slack, HubSpot) and your legacy systems.

How do AI agents reduce manual work?

AI agents work autonomously to complete tasks that would normally require human intervention. They can read emails, extract data, make decisions based on rules, and take actions across multiple systems. A single agent can replace 35+ hours of manual work per week.

Is n8n better than Zapier for automation?

n8n is often preferred for complex business automation because it offers self-hosting (data stays on your servers), unlimited workflow executions, and advanced logic capabilities. Zapier is simpler for basic automations but becomes expensive at scale.

How much does AI automation cost and what is the ROI?

Our Starter workflows begin at $497 as a one-time setup fee. The ROI is usually measured in weeks. If an agent saves you 10 hours a week, and your time is worth $50/hr, the system pays for itself in just one month. After that, it is pure profit.

Can AI agents handle customer support?

Yes! Modern AI agents can handle 70%+ of customer support tickets autonomously. They use your knowledge base to answer questions accurately, escalating complex issues to humans. Response times drop from hours to seconds.

What industries benefit most from AI automation?

Any industry with repetitive data tasks benefits: Real Estate (lead qualification), Finance (invoice processing), HR (resume screening), SaaS (customer onboarding), Agencies (lead generation), and E-commerce (order management).

How long does it take to build an automation?

Simple workflows take 1-3 days. Complex enterprise integrations typically require 2-4 weeks. We start with a discovery call to map your processes, then build, test, and deploy the automation.

Is my data secure with AI automation?

Absolutely. We use "Siloed Architecture" where your data never leaves your private cloud instance. We self-host n8n on your infrastructure, ensuring complete data sovereignty. Your data is never used to train external AI models.

What is the difference between AI Agents and Agentic AI?

"AI Agent" typically refers to the specific software entity (e.g., a Customer Support Agent), while "Agentic AI" refers to the broader technological shift from Generative AI (chatbots) to Autonomous AI (systems that act). We specialize in building Agentic workflows.

Are there courses to learn about AI Agents?

Yes. For deep technical skills, we recommend the Google "Generative AI for Developers" path or DeepLearning.AI courses. However, Jento AI allows you to skip the learning curve by deploying pre-built, enterprise-grade agents instantly.
